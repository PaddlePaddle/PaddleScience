# Co-creation Plan

As an open source project, PaddleScience welcomes partners from all walks of life to jointly build top open source projects in the AI for Science field based on PaddlePaddle, create an active and forward-looking AI for Science open source community, establish a closed loop of industry-university-research, and promote scientific research innovation and industrial empowerment. Click to learn about [PaddlePaddle AI for Science Co-creation Plan](https://www.paddlepaddle.org.cn/science)
